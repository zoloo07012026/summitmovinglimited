Moving Checklist

What to prepare before the crew arrives.

1 week before

  • ✓Confirm elevator or parking access
  • ✓Separate items you will carry yourself
  • ✓Label rooms clearly

1 day before

  • ✓Clear walkways and entry points
  • ✓Pack personal essentials
  • ✓Defrost or disconnect appliances if needed

Move day

  • ✓Walk the crew through priorities
  • ✓Point out fragile or specialty items
  • ✓Review final room placement

Packing Guide

Start with low-use items, keep boxes room-specific, and avoid overpacking heavy boxes.

  • ✓Use small boxes for books and dense items
  • ✓Wrap glassware and dishes individually
  • ✓Mark fragile boxes on multiple sides
  • ✓Keep medications and documents with you

Building Access Notes

A few access details can prevent delays and extra stress on move day.

  • ✓Reserve elevators where required
  • ✓Confirm loading dock or street parking rules
  • ✓Measure tight stairways or hallways
  • ✓Share gate codes or concierge instructions
