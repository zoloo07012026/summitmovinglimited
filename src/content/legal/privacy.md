## Information we collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect the information you provide, including name, phone number, email address, moving addresses or areas, move date, move size, and move details.

## How we use information

We use quote request information to respond to your inquiry, estimate your move, schedule services, prevent spam, and maintain operational records.

## Storage and access

Quote requests may be stored in the website database and may be emailed to the business when notifications are configured. Access should be limited to authorized staff.

## Security

The website uses form validation, basic rate limiting where configured, and reasonable administrative safeguards. No website can guarantee absolute security.

## Third-party services

The site may use an email provider for notifications, analytics tools, and spam prevention services when configured.
